# VOLUMETRIC · roadmap

## Current delivery

Phase 0 and functional flow of Phase 1 implemented. Independent Rust engine, seven primitive, real OBJ and STL binary/ASCII and tapered diagnostics; orbital orthographic camera and model transformations; real intersections with X/Y/Z planes or custom direction; junction of contours, components and coplanar cases; number/space/slice range; dual or individual view; vertical/paced A3 composition; inks per function; placement in Design and SVG export by the existing system. Independent recipes with embedded mesh allow the calculation to be repeated.

Parts of the 2  –  3 phases are advanced: camera depth/distance fields, analytical occlusion, main/secondary, ATLAS and STRATA, and initial X-RAY structural extraction. This is not the same as ending those phases.

## The following priorities

1. 2 phase: camera with accurate views at the ends and perspective with close plane clipping; unplug it without changing generators. Curvature, illumination as a field for engraving and combinations of fields, conscious surface smoothing, non-uniform intervals, reproducible deformation and view of section planes. Extend measurements of large models and evaluate BVH for Original mode.
2. 3 phase: explicit classification by type, discontinuous occult stroke in its own ink and overlapping structure with topography. Current X-RAY allows everything/only visible, without separating hidden edges by style. Silhouettes depend on coherent norms; topographic occlusion does not.
3. Persistence: groups of 3D recipes linked to the objects, with editing from Layers, duplication and repainting of inks integrated into the document. Independent JSON recipes and fixed traces are currently stored in `.vectorio`.
4. Import: GLB/glTF with transformations, units, hierarchy and materials. OBJ and STL have no units: Z orientation above and explicit normalization, with XYZ adjustments. Imported materials/textures/norms and STL attribute colors are ignored; objects retain names and belonging but are interpreted together.
5. 4: engraving by tangent fields and surface illumination. ENGRAVING does not appear as an empty preset.
6. Phase 5: RUIN, PARALLAX, multiview, multiple composition, seeds with defined artistic effects and controlled variation. The seed is stored in parameters, without any random effects during this delivery.
7. Optional 6 phase: metadata and AI suggestions, always with local geometric operation.

## Current limits

- Upper chamber: elevation 0.01 ° –  89.99 °, with no lower views or perspective.
- Reference 3D viewfinder by order of depth, without object selection, mesh editing, z-buffer or manipulable plans. The overlapping curves show the entire family to understand the section; the 2D view shows the final visibility.
- There is adjustable geometric simplification of work and the original is preserved. The import limits are now 512 MB and 5 million triangles. Open and non-manifold meshes produce warnings; no holes are fixed automatically.
- Recipes and controls are kept during the session; before closing, save the recipe to preserve the 3D font. They are not incorporated into the automatic recovery of the project.
- A3 and cut are tested; SVG syntactic compatibility is validated with usvg. The opening in Illustrator/Inkscape is recorded separately only if it is actually verified.
- No moves are sent to any drawbot.


## Expansion after initial use

Implemented global OBJ/STL drag to Solo 3D, expanded boundaries to 512 MB/ 5 million triangles, STL vertices compaction and three levels of detail. The flow is tested with real local models of 209,872 and 1,499,605 triangles. The original is preserved; the interactive quality produces an explicit approximate interpretation. Additional optimization pending: Original mode with millions of faces (especially its occlusion), reduction percentage progress and resumption of very large recipes.


## Artistic language: first integration

FLAT SHADOW implemented: orientable light, own shadow by orientation and actual occlusion of light, shadow on the floor, faceted mesh retaining the source and a shared ink. Mass or trajectory view; optional second tone and contour. The SVG contains continuous vector hatching by region. Persistence of parameters in recipes. Directional engraving continues as the next step. Geodesic waves, chromatic mesh and combination of families are integrated into the next enlargement.


## Three integrated surface languages · 16 the month of November 2026

WAVES DE SUPERFICIE, MESH CROMATIC and WAVES + MESH implemented with contextual controls, inks per family, recipes, SVG and placement on canvas. Waves per graph distance with origin per component, seeded deformation and interference from two ends. Artistically triangulated mesh of adjustable complexity, one/three inks per orientation/height/depth/object. Combined with waves and up to two independent structural inks. The original is preserved and the paper uses the occlusion of the drawn surface itself. Pendants: flow through tangent/curvature fields, multiple source points edited on the viewfinder and 3D fonts linked from the document.
